My Story

Curiosity, formalized.

The path from curiosity to CTF wins, and where I'm headed next.

Bio

Blessing Ndoro is a Cybersecurity student at Southeast Missouri State University with a focus on digital forensics and incident response. A first-place solo Capture the Flag winner and a selected BETA tester for World Wide Technology's CTF challenges, Blessing pairs hands-on offensive thinking with the patience of a forensic investigator — piecing together the story behind a breach rather than just patching the hole.

Outside the terminal, Blessing serves as President of the SEMO Chess Club and as a Senior Ambassador for the Office of Admissions, and has applied that same strategic thinking to hackathon work, building an AI-powered fall-detection and care platform from scratch. Blessing is currently working toward CompTIA Security+ and CHFI certifications, sharpening a focus on forensics-driven security work.

My Journey

Fall 2023 — Present

The Beginning: SEMO & a Calling Toward Cybersecurity

I started at Southeast Missouri State University in Fall 2023, drawn to cybersecurity by one simple thing: curiosity. In a world where threats evolve daily, I wanted a skill set that would constantly challenge me to think like an attacker and stay one step ahead. It felt less like choosing a major and more like answering a calling.

Oct 2023 — Present

Leading On and Off the Board

Alongside coursework, I took on leadership roles that mirror the strategic thinking cybersecurity demands. As President of the Chess Club, I get to sharpen the same pattern-recognition and long-range planning I use in security work. As a Senior Ambassador for the Office of Admissions, I help guide the next generation of SEMO students through their own beginnings.

2024 — Present

Testing Myself: CTFs and Real Competition

Coursework only goes so far, so I started competing. I took first place solo in a Capture the Flag competition hosted by Cyber Pro AI, working through reverse-engineering challenges and scripting my way past a privilege-escalation puzzle most competitors couldn't crack. Soon after, I was selected as a BETA tester for new CTF challenges built by World Wide Technology — getting an early look at how the next generation of security challenges gets designed.

Summer 2025 (Jun — Aug)

Into the Field: Missouri Cybersecurity Center of Excellence

My first real taste of enterprise security work came as a Cybersecurity Intern with the Missouri Cybersecurity Center of Excellence. I helped assess a city's IT infrastructure — deploying SentinelOne and ConnectWise RMM, documenting assets, and running ransomware threat analysis. I also got hands-on with penetration testing, HTB labs, and SOC operations: log monitoring, threat detection, and incident response, all grounded in NIST, ISO 27001, and Cloud Control Matrix frameworks.

Aug 2025 — Present

Building While Learning: IT Help Desk & AI

Since August 2025, I've worked as an IT Help Desk Technician at SEMO, troubleshooting real problems for real people across Windows, macOS, and mobile — a different kind of pressure than a CTF, but just as valuable. In parallel, I built ALA, an AI-powered fall-detection and care platform, full-stack — from the alert escalation pipeline to the AI adapter routing — proving to myself I could bring a security-minded approach to a completely different kind of system.

Present — Future

Where I'm Headed Next

I'm currently working toward my CompTIA Security+ and CHFI certifications, sharpening my focus on the forensics side of security — the part of this field where I get to piece together the story behind an incident instead of just responding to it. I'm looking for an internship where I can bring that mindset into a real SOC or forensics team, and keep building from there.
